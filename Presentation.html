The Monolithic Problem

Modern global universities outgrow traditional, single-server SQL architectures. They suffer from:

  • Data Fragmentation: Campuses operate in silos.
  • Performance Bottlenecks: Syncing 50,000+ profiles crashes legacy apps.
  • Vendor Lock-in: Tied to a single backend language (e.g., legacy PHP).
  • Poor Visibility: Executives lack real-time financial and asset metrics.

The Monolith Fails

High latency, difficult to scale, and prone to "Commands out of sync" errors when subjected to massive load.
